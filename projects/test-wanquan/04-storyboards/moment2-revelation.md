# 分镜方案：Moment 2 - 老太太死了

> 片段编号：片段2-A
> 总时长：15秒
> 镜头数量：4格
> 出场角色：沈晚宁、林浅浅、顾砚、顾思敏、张经理
> 场景：万豪宴会厅

---

## 分镜图Prompt

### 【片段2-A | 0s–15s】

**【第1格】0.0s–4.0s**
- 景别：Medium Shot
- 运镜：Static
- 场景参考：宴会厅中景
> 本格时长：4s，林浅浅得意洋洋地说话

- 画面：林浅浅站在沈晚宁面前，表情得意，嘴角上扬，双手交叠胸前，一副看好戏的模样
- 光影：顶光，略显冷调
- 色调：冷色调
- 氛围：得意、嚣张
- 图生图输入：林浅浅角色参考 + 宴会厅场景参考图
- 图生图Prompt：
```
Medium shot of a young woman in a cream white dress standing confidently 
with arms crossed, smirking victoriously at someone off-camera,
overhead lighting with cool undertones, luxurious hotel banquet hall background,
cinematic atmosphere, no text, no watermarks
```

---

**【第2格】4.0s–8.0s**
- 景别：Close-up
- 运镜：Push in（推进）
- 场景参考：宴会厅中景
> 本格时长：4s，沈晚宁的冷静反应

- 画面：沈晚宁的特写镜头，表情平静，甚至带着一丝冷笑，眼神里有一种"你们不知道真相"的优越感
- 光影：侧光，深邃
- 色调：冷色调
- 氛围：冷静、压制、嘲讽
- 图生图输入：沈晚宁角色参考 + 宴会厅场景参考图
- 图生图Prompt：
```
Extreme close-up of a composed woman with long black hair in a dark gray blazer,
her expression is eerily calm with a hint of a cold smirk,
she looks directly at the camera with piercing eyes conveying 
a sense of knowing something others don't,
dramatic side lighting, cool cinematic tones, no text, no watermarks
```

---

**【第3格】8.0s–12.0s**
- 景别：Medium Shot
- 运镜：Tracking（跟拍）
- 场景参考：宴会厅中景
> 本格时长：4s，张经理急匆匆闯入

- 画面：一位穿着西装的男性（张经理）从画面左侧急匆匆跑入，表情惊慌，额头冒汗，边跑边喊
- 光影：运动模糊暗示速度
- 色调：暖色（与前面冷调形成对比）
- 氛围：惊慌、急迫
- 图生图输入：张经理角色参考 + 宴会厅场景参考图
- 图生图Prompt：
```
Medium shot of a panicked middle-aged man in a black suit rushing 
into frame from the left, his expression is shocked and urgent,
slight motion blur to suggest speed, forehead glistening with sweat,
he appears to be rushing to deliver urgent news,
luxurious hotel banquet hall background, warm cinematic lighting, no text
```

---

**【第4格】12.0s–15.0s**
- 景别：Wide Shot
- 运镜：Static
- 场景参考：宴会厅大景
> 本格时长：3s，众人震惊的群像反应

- 画面：全景镜头，所有人都停下动作，望向画面中心，表情震惊，面面相觑
- 光影：顶光
- 色调：中性偏冷
- 氛围：震惊、凝固
- 图生图输入：宴会厅大场景参考图
- 图生图Prompt：
```
Wide shot of a luxurious hotel banquet hall, all the well-dressed guests 
have stopped mid-conversation and turned to look toward something off-camera,
expressions range from shocked to confused, wide-eyed stares,
overhead crystal chandeliers casting warm light on the scene,
cinematic documentary style, no text, no watermarks
```
